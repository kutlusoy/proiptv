ProIPTV plays the streams of your own IPTV provider on phones, tablets and Android TV. No content is included, there is no account with us, no ads and no analytics.

<b>Sources</b>
• Xtream Codes accounts and M3U playlists with XMLTV guides, several accounts side by side
• Passwords are encrypted with the Android Keystore

<b>Features</b>
• Live TV with groups, search, now and next, zapping and number keys
• Program guide that keeps itself current, catch-up
• Movies and series with info pages, seasons, progress and watched marks
• Favorites with several lists and manual sorting
• Pause and rewind live TV, recording and scheduled recordings
• Downloads for offline use
• Set up accounts with the phone by QR code
• TV layout with remote control navigation
• Backup and restore of accounts, favorites and settings
• Eleven languages
